Athens and the coast

Areas

Neighbourhoods where Asi already has investors, and the coast outside the city.

  • Pangrati

    A sought-after neighbourhood by the centre and the Panathenaic Stadium, with cafés and local life.

  • Kypseli

    A lively area north of the centre, with older buildings and the green Fokionos Negri promenade.

  • Exarchia

    A student and arts district in the centre, near the universities and the Archaeological Museum.

  • Neos Kosmos

    South of the Acropolis and close to Koukaki, with a metro station.

  • Nea Smyrni

    A southern family suburb with a large central square and busy shops.

  • Kallithea

    A large urban area between central Athens and the sea, with metro and tram nearby.

  • Zografou

    A residential and student area by the university campus, at the foot of Mount Hymettus.

  • Vyronas

    A residential neighbourhood at the foot of Hymettus, close to Pangrati.

  • Kaisariani

    A neighbourhood at the foot of Hymettus, with its own history and Kaisariani Monastery nearby.

  • Gyzi

    A neighbourhood near the centre and Pedion tou Areos park.

  • Patissia

    An urban area north of the centre, along Patission Street and the metro line.

  • Kolonos

    An established residential neighbourhood north-west of the centre.

  • Peristeri

    A large municipality in western Athens, on the metro.

  • Nea Chalkidona

    A residential area in north-western Athens.

Nea Makri
