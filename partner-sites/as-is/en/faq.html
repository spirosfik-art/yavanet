Questions

What everyone asks before buying.

Can I meet Asi in Israel?

Yes. Asi travels constantly between Israel and Greece, so you can meet near you in Israel, or in Greece with a tour of properties.

Do I have to deal with the Greek bureaucracy?

No. The Greek team of lawyers, notaries and accountants handles it, and Asi guides you through.

I already own a flat in Greece. Can you help?

Yes. Asi lists the property on all the Greek property sites, finds quality tenants and prepares the lease. Management is done with a local team, in partnership with S.F. Properties.

Do you only work in Athens?

Asi works in every neighbourhood of Athens, with apartments and villas, and on the coast outside the city, such as Oropos and Nea Makri.

What types of rental are possible?

Long-term, mid-term, or short-term as a holiday home on Airbnb. It depends on the property, the area and your goal.

What is an off-market deal?

A property that wasn't published on property sites, and comes through the connections Asi has built over years of work in Athens.

Do I need to come to Greece for a renovation?

Not necessarily. Asi is in Athens all the time and sees every renovation up close.
