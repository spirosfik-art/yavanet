Golden Visa · Greece

It meets both conditions

In Attica, the real-estate route to the Golden Visa requires at least €800,000 in a single property and at least 120 m² of main areas. Storage and parking do not count.

→  5-year residence permit, renewable while you own the property
→  Covers spouse, children under 21 and both sets of parents
→  No minimum stay in Greece required
→  The property may not be let as a short-term rental
